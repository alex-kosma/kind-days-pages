  • Registration of a new device may require a pairing code.
  • Do not abuse the service: no automated or scripted requests, no attempts to bypass the limits, extract credentials, or access another device’s record, and no use of the coach to break the law or harm others.
  • We may throttle, suspend, or revoke a device registration that abuses the service. Because there are no accounts, this applies per device.
  • If you enter your own Anthropic API key in Settings, coaching requests go from your phone straight to Anthropic on your own account and your own bill; our server and its ceiling are not involved. You are then responsible for that key, for the charges it incurs, and for using it within Anthropic’s own terms.
