Common questions

Where is my data? Is it backed up?

On your phone, and no. Kind Days has no account and no cloud sync, so nothing is backed up anywhere by us. Uninstalling the app deletes everything permanently.

Use More → Settings → Export data before changing phones or reinstalling. That file is a complete copy, and More → Settings → Import data restores it on the new device. Importing the same file twice is safe — it will not create duplicates.

The coach says “Network request failed” or never replies

The coach is the only feature that needs a server; everything else works offline. Check Settings → Check connection. If it fails, the server address is wrong or unreachable — the correct address goes in Settings → Server URL.

The coach says its time is used up, or that it is taking a break

Each device has a daily and a monthly ceiling on coaching, to keep costs bounded, and the service as a whole has a daily one. The daily ceilings refill once a day and the monthly one on the 1st, on our server’s clock — a few hours off your own midnight, depending on where you are. “Taking a break” means the service-wide ceiling was reached, not yours. Everything else in the app keeps working.

The microphone button disappeared

That is your phone reporting no speech recogniser available, which can come and go across restarts and varies by device. Typing always works, everywhere the microphone would have.

A reminder arrived late

Reminders are an invitation, not an appointment. Android in particular may deliver them inside a window rather than at the exact minute, to save battery.

I want to delete everything

Settings → Erase all data. It clears the on-device database and cancels any scheduled reminders. Uninstalling does the same thing.

Can I get my data out?

Yes — More → Settings → Export data. It is a plain file you own and can read.

What this app deliberately does not do

Some of these are asked for often enough to be worth stating: there are no streaks, no scores, no “days missed”, and no notification that tells you what you failed to do. Repeated things are designed to survive a bad week and never reset. That is intentional, not a missing feature.
