Kind Days

The journal that never scolds. One line a day about what got done — or what was simply good — and a coach that reads it back to you when you ask. No streaks, no scores, and no record anywhere of the days you missed.

Everything you write stays on your phone. Asking the coach sends only what it needs to answer, and nothing is kept. The coach is free during early access.
