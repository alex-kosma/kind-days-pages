Kind Days — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 September 2026.

Kind Days is built local-first. Your journal, goals, wins, decisions, plans and coaching transcripts live in a database on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, and no copy of your life on a server.

This policy describes the one place that changes: talking to the coach.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter. Profile, goals, the log, wins, decisions, plans, reminder settings, language and theme preferences, and the full text of every coaching conversation. None of it is uploaded, backed up, or synced anywhere by Kind Days. It is not readable by us, because it never reaches us.

If you uninstall the app, that data is deleted with it. There is no way to recover it — use More → Export to keep a copy first. “Erase all data” in Settings clears the same database on demand.

What happens when you use the coach

Coaching replies are generated by a large language model, which cannot run on a phone. When you send a message to the coach, the app sends to our server:

  • your message, and the earlier messages in that conversation;
  • a selected slice of your own data, chosen by the kind of session — this can include your profile, goal text, recent log entries, recent wins, open decisions, your current plan, and one-line summaries of past sessions.

Our server adds one thing — a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your device’s random identifier, so that misuse can be tied to a single device rather than to everyone using the app — and immediately forwards this to Anthropic, whose model writes the reply. That scrambled identifier cannot be turned back into the device record, and it is not linked to your name, email, phone, or Apple/Google account, because we never receive those. The reply streams back to your phone and is stored on your phone.

Our server does not store any of it. It holds the request only for as long as it takes to pass it on and stream the answer back.

Anthropic processes this content in order to generate the reply, under their own terms: anthropic.com/legal/privacy

If you never open the coach, nothing about you leaves your phone at all.

If you use your own key

Settings lets you enter your own Anthropic API key. When you do, the app sends coaching requests directly from your phone to Anthropic, on your own account and your own bill. Our server is not involved at all: it never sees the request, and none of the device record or daily counters described below are created. What Anthropic receives is the same as above — your message and the selected slice of your data, without the scrambled device identifier — under the same terms.

What our server does keep

Two things, neither of which contains anything you wrote:

  • A device record. A random identifier and a hashed access token created the first time the app talks to the server, plus the dates it was created and last used. It is not linked to your name, email, phone, or Apple/Google account, because we never receive those.
  • Daily counters. Per device and per day: how many coach requests were made and how many tokens they used. This exists to cap runaway costs and abuse.

Your IP address is used momentarily to rate-limit sign-ups and is not written to disk.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No profiling beyond what the coach reads to answer you.

Dictation

If you dictate instead of typing, audio is handled by your operating system’s speech recognition, not by Kind Days. Depending on your device and its settings, Apple or Google may process that audio on the device or on their servers under their own privacy terms. Kind Days receives only the resulting text, and never records, stores, or transmits audio itself.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. They are not push notifications; nothing is sent from a server, and no notification token leaves the device.

Children

Kind Days is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Since we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing to delete on request — but see below.

Your control over your data

Because your data is on your device and our server holds nothing personal:

  • Delete everything: Settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Export everything: More → Export, which produces a file you own.
  • Delete the device record: it contains no personal information, but if you want it removed anyway, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
